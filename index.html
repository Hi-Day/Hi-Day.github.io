About

Dr. Hidayaturrahman, S.Kom., M.T. is a faculty member in the School of Computer Science at BINUS University (Staff ID D6423), serving as Concentration Content Coordinator for Intelligent Systems and Leader of the BINUS Agentic-AI Hub (Agentic AI Center, Innovation Award 2025). His BRIDGE research profile lists keywords across computer vision, NLP, recommendation systems, generative AI, and deep learning, with a hands-on stack of Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and OpenCV.

His research sits at the intersection of language, vision, learning, and adaptation: contextual language models for argumentation mining (J. Big Data 2021, most-cited), data augmentation for sarcasm detection, neural style transfer accelerated with knowledge distillation (IEEE AIDAS 2022), and — most recently — adversarial multitask learning with a domain adapter plus catastrophic-forgetting studies on Office-31 (2× IEEE Access 2024, doctoral research on domain adaptation, deep learning, and multitask learning). He also publishes applied reviews on mobile-health engagement and tourism UI/UX.

Beyond papers, he is a visible science communicator and builder: a 100+ attendee NUNI national seminar on “Style Transfer — How AI Can Paint Like an Artist” (2021), an industry workshop on AutoCopywriting with AI with PT Pharos Indonesia (2022), an introductory ML essay widely cited by student work (SoCS 2020), and the BINUS Gallery product FLAVORFUL. He mentors undergraduate research — several student co-authors (Emmanuel Dave, Alif Tri Handoyo, Samuel Philip) appear on his papers — and collaborates with senior BINUS professors including Derwin Suhartono, Agung Trisetyarso, Iman H. Kartowisastro, Widodo Budiharto, Harco Warnars, Benfano Soewito, and Ford Lumban Gaol.
